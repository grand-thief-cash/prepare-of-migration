# European IT English Roadmap

## 面向欧洲 IT / Software Engineering 工作的英语听说训练体系

> 目标不是“把英语学好”。
>
> 目标是：
>
> **能够在欧洲全英文 IT 团队中工作、面试、讨论技术、参加会议，并能够在压力下清晰表达自己的技术观点。**

---

# 1. 最终目标

## 1.1 不以 IELTS / TOEFL 分数作为唯一目标

对于 IT 工程师来说，真正重要的是：

```text
听懂别人
    ↓
理解技术上下文
    ↓
快速形成自己的观点
    ↓
用英语表达
    ↓
接受追问
    ↓
解释 trade-off
    ↓
参加技术讨论
    ↓
参加 System Design
    ↓
完成真实工作沟通
```

最终目标：

> **International Professional English**

而不是：

> Native English

# 3. 最核心的两个能力

## 3.1 Listening

最终要求：

```text
正常技术会议
        ↓
能听懂 80–90%
        ↓
不同国家口音
        ↓
不依赖字幕
        ↓
能够理解上下文
        ↓
能够抓住真正的问题
```

不要追求：

> 每一个单词都听懂。

而要追求：

> **即使漏掉几个词，我仍然知道对方是什么意思。**

---

# 4. Listening 能力分层

## Level 1 — 慢速理解

目标：

* 熟悉英文发音
* 熟悉技术词汇

素材：

* Go tutorials
* AWS tutorials
* Kafka tutorials
* 简单 system design

目标理解率：

> 90%+

---

## Level 2 — 正常技术英语

素材：

* Engineering talks
* Conference talks
* Architecture discussions
* Technical podcasts

目标：

> 80–90%

---

## Level 3 — 自然语速

素材：

* Conference interviews
* Engineering discussion
* Podcast
* Tech leadership discussion

目标：

> 75–85%

---

## Level 4 — 国际团队英语

加入：

* German accent
* Indian accent
* French accent
* Dutch accent
* British accent
* American accent

目标：

> 即使口音不同，也能够理解核心内容。

# 5. Technical Listening 的训练方法

每天一个材料，采用：

```text
第一遍
↓
不看字幕
↓
理解总体意思

第二遍
↓
英文字幕
↓
确认没听懂的内容

第三遍
↓
暂停
↓
跟读

第四遍
↓
关闭字幕
↓
再次听
```

---

# 6. Shadowing

Shadowing 是核心训练。

例：

视频：

> The main reason we chose Kafka was to decouple the producers from the consumers.

暂停。

自己重复：

> The main reason we chose Kafka was to decouple the producers from the consumers.

训练：

* pronunciation
* rhythm
* intonation
* speaking speed
* sentence structure
* automaticity

---

# 7. 不要只练单词，要练“句子块”

不要只背：

```text
trade-off
latency
throughput
bottleneck
consistency
scalability
```

而要学习：

```text
The main trade-off is...

The main bottleneck is...

The system starts showing higher latency when...

This approach scales better because...

The downside is...

I'm mainly concerned about...

We eventually found that...

The root cause was...

```

这些句子才是真正的工作英语。

---

# 8. Speaking 的最终目标

不是：

> Can you speak English?

而是：

> Can you think and explain in English?

---

# 9. Speaking 四个阶段

## Stage 1 — 复述

听完一个技术视频：

> 用英语讲出视频内容。

时间：

> 2–3 分钟

---

## Stage 2 — Explanation

选择一个技术主题：

> Explain Kafka consumer groups.

要求：

> 连续讲 5 分钟。

---

## Stage 3 — Opinion

例如：

> Would you choose Kafka or RabbitMQ?

要求：

```text
观点
↓
理由
↓
Trade-off
↓
反例
↓
结论
```

---

## Stage 4 — Pressure

别人开始连续问：

```text
Why?
Why not?
What if?
How?
What happens when...?
How would you improve it?
```

你依然能够回答。

这才意味着进入工作级英语。

# 10. Technical Speaking 模板

## 表达观点

```text
I think...

I'd argue that...

From my perspective...

Personally, I'd prefer...

My main concern is...
```

---

## 表达原因

```text
The main reason is...

We chose this because...

The main consideration was...

This was mainly driven by...
```

---

## 表达 Trade-off

```text
The main advantage is...

The downside is...

The trade-off here is...

This comes at the cost of...

The benefit is..., but the downside is...
```

---

## 表达不确定

```text
I'm not entirely sure, but...

My understanding is that...

As far as I know...

I'd need to verify that.

I haven't worked with this directly, but...
```

---

## 请求澄清

```text
Could you clarify what you mean by...?

Do you mean...?

If I understand correctly...

Just to make sure I understand the question...
```

---

## 不同意

```text
I see your point, but...

I'm not sure I completely agree with that.

I think there's another consideration here.

I'd approach this slightly differently.
```

---

## 思考时间

```text
Let me think about that for a second.

There are a couple of things I'd consider here.

I'd probably approach this in two steps.

The first thing I'd look at is...
```

# 11. Interview English

必须能够自然回答以下问题。

## Background

```text
Tell me about yourself.

Walk me through your current role.

What are you currently working on?
```

## Project

```text
Tell me about your most challenging project.

What was your contribution?

What was the biggest technical challenge?
```

## Architecture

```text
How did you design the system?

Why did you choose this architecture?

What alternatives did you consider?
```

## Technical decision

```text
Why did you choose Go?

Why Kafka?

Why Flink?

Why PostgreSQL?

Why AWS?
```

## Production

```text
What went wrong?

How did you debug it?

What was the root cause?

How did you prevent it from happening again?
```

## Performance

```text
How did you improve performance?

What was the bottleneck?

What metrics did you monitor?
```

## Distributed Systems

```text
How did you handle consistency?

How did you handle failures?

How did you deal with retries?

What happens if a service crashes?
```

# 13. Workplace English

必须训练真实工作场景。

## Stand-up

```text
Yesterday I worked on...

Today I'm going to...

I'm currently blocked by...
```

## Code Review

```text
I think we should change this because...

I'm concerned about...

Could we simplify this?

Would it make sense to...
```

## Architecture Meeting

```text
I see two possible approaches.

The first option is...

The main advantage is...

However, the downside is...

Personally, I'd prefer...
```

## Incident

```text
We started seeing elevated latency around 10 AM.

The first thing I checked was...

Eventually, we found that...

The root cause was...

We mitigated the issue by...

The follow-up action is...
```

# 17. 能力验收

## Listening

随机选择 10–20 分钟技术视频：

目标：

> 不开字幕理解 80–90%。

---

## Speaking

随机选择：

> Kafka / Go / AWS / Flink

连续：

> 5 分钟

不使用中文。

---

## Interview

进行 45–60 分钟英文面试。

要求：

* 听懂问题
* 直接回答
* 能接受追问
* 可以解释 trade-off
* 不需要频繁切换中文

---

## System Design

随机：

> Design a high-throughput event processing system.

要求：

> 英语讲 20–30 分钟。
